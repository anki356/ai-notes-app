# Project Summary: AI Notes App

## Business Objective
To provide a privacy-first, high-performance note-taking application that empowers users with advanced AI capabilities (summarization, semantic search, voice transcription) running locally or with secure integrations, ensuring data ownership and productivity.

## Problem Statement
Traditional note-taking apps often fall into two categories:
1.  **Simple & Local:** Private but lack intelligent features like automated organization or summarization.
2.  **AI-Powered & Cloud:** Feature-rich but require uploading sensitive personal or professional data to third-party servers, raising privacy concerns.

Users need a solution that bridges this gap, providing "brain-like" search and synthesis without the privacy trade-off.

## Target Users
- **Knowledge Workers/Researchers:** Who need to connect disparate ideas across large volumes of notes.
- **Students:** Who want quick summaries of lectures and efficient search through study materials.
- **Developers:** Who prefer Markdown-based workflows and local-first tools.
- **Privacy-Conscious Professionals:** Who handle sensitive information and cannot use cloud-based AI tools.

## Success Metrics
- **Performance:** Local semantic search results delivered in < 200ms.
- **Accuracy:** AI summaries rated as "helpful" by 80% of users in beta testing.
- **Privacy:** 100% of core AI operations (embeddings, search) capable of running offline.
- **Engagement:** High daily active usage (DAU) and growth in the number of notes linked via semantic search.
